One line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to
the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## Entry

> Four billion years of wishes, and not one of you has ever wished me a happy birthday.

85 characters, one line. The Moon is wry and old and a little tired of being
wished upon — four billion years of granting wishes, and nobody ever wished
anything back. The turn lands in one read: the Moon is not angry, it is just
mildly, cosmically unappreciated.

```sh
node main.ts
```
